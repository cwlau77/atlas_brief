Key figures on development cards

Every figure below is real. Haiku pulled it from the titles and snippets of live articles on 27 October, and Atlas found it word for word in the article it cites. The card text around the figures is shortened. Option A (rows) is the one I recommend. Option B (tiles) comes at the end for comparison.

A1 · comparable figures → bars

Three counts of people, from three outlets

All three are counts of people, so they share one scale from zero. Each bar is labelled with the article's own words, including "Nearly". The caption says the figures come from different outlets and dates.

B · alternative: stat tiles

The same Sudan figures as tiles

Tiles give the numbers more weight, but at card width three long figures ("34 million people") crowd the row. Short bars inside a tile are harder to compare than one shared column of bars. On phones the tiles stack and end up as rows anyway.
