# Hotspots Page Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Ship `/hotspots`, a flat pixel-grid world map of which countries today's world news names most. Hovering a country lifts it and opens a card with the most widely covered story. The page costs $0 to run.

**Architecture:** A new backend module counts country mentions in the 14 world-desk RSS feeds. Matching is deterministic, from a gazetteer CSV. The module keeps a rolling 24-hour article store and a one-hour, stale-while-revalidate snapshot cache in process, and serves the snapshot at `GET /hotspots`. The frontend draws the map from a grid of country cells precomputed at build time (no geo library at runtime), styles it from a continuous heat ramp held in `tokens.css`, and builds the page from small pure helpers (scale, layout, cache) plus React components.

**Tech Stack:** FastAPI, feedparser, httpx, pytest + respx; React 19, react-router 7, plain SVG, vitest; `flag-icons` (MIT) for 4:3 flag SVGs.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-27-hotspots-design.md`. Read it before starting any task. The mockup `docs/superpowers/specs/assets/2026-10-27-hotspots-mockup.html` is the visual and motion reference: its `setHover`, `score` and `backGrid` functions are the reference behaviour.

## Global Constraints

- **No LLM calls anywhere in this feature.** Running cost is $0 (owner: "too expensive").
- **Sources:** the 14 world desks only: BBC World, Al Jazeera, The Guardian World, NPR World, The Hindu, Deutsche Welle, France 24, AllAfrica, Straits Times, CNN World, Fox News World, New York Times World, ABC News International, CBS News World. No keyed APIs, no GDELT, no Google News.
- The shared briefing path (`rss_source.fetch`, `_parse_feed`, `_entry_to_article`) is unchanged.
- Keep the `httpx`/`httpcore` loggers at WARNING (`backend/main.py`).
- The backend stays a single process. In-memory caches only.
- Every colour lives in `frontend/src/styles/tokens.css`. TS copies of hex values must be pinned to `tokens.css` by a test, the same way route colours are in `palette.test.ts`.
- Heat ramp stops (Lab interpolation): `--heat-0 #5E9F6E` (t=0), `--heat-1 #A3BF63` (.25), `--heat-2 #E2BE4E` (.45), `--heat-3 #E08A2A` (.62), `--heat-4 #E2381B` (.80), `--heat-5 #A3200B` (1.0). Green appears only on this ramp and its swatches.
- Tier text: High `--hot-ink`, Elevated `--warm-ink`, Active and Quiet `--ink-2`. Text never wears ramp colours.
- Lift curve: `cubic-bezier(0.2, 0.8, 0.2, 1)`. All motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`; under it, the final state appears instantly.
- **Nothing loops:** no pulses, ripples (other than the single ring when the point lands), drag or breathing. No name tags on the map. No outlines on the card or legend (corner brackets plus a soft shadow).
- IBM Plex Sans only; tabular figures for numbers.
- Saved briefings in localStorage keep rendering; nothing here touches their shape.
- Commit messages end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`.
- Verification commands: `.venv/bin/python -m pytest backend/tests -q` (from the repo root); in `frontend/`: `npx vitest run`, `npx tsc -b`, `npm run lint` (3 pre-existing warnings are fine), `npm run build`.

## Review Focus

1. **A feed returns a 200 with garbage or HTML.** Expect it to be skipped and logged, with the snapshot built from the rest. Test in Task 2: `test_unparseable_feed_yields_no_articles`.
2. **Ambiguous words match the wrong thing** ("turkey dinner", "Georgia governor", "Michael Jordan", "Chad Smith", "Washington state"). Expect no country counted. Tests in Task 1.
3. **Every feed fails on a cold start.** Expect a 503 with nothing cached, and the frontend's empty state with Retry, never a crash or a blank map. Tests in Task 4 (`test_cold_failure_returns_503`) and Task 10 (`renders the unavailable state with retry`).
4. **localStorage throws** (private mode or full storage) or holds an old or malformed snapshot. Expect the page to fetch and render normally. Test in Task 6: `ignores a malformed or throwing cache`.
5. **A country is in the snapshot but has no cells on the map** (Singapore, Bahrain, Kosovo). Expect it in the list, and hovering or pinning its row opens a docked card with no lift or leader. Test in Task 9: `country without cells opens a docked card`.

- [ ] **Step 3: Build `countries.csv` and implement `countries_named(text: str) -> set[str]`**

Approach: compile one alternation regex of all terms, longest first, with `(?<![\w-])…(?![\w-])` boundaries, case-sensitive (proper nouns), plus a case-sensitive possessive allowance (`Jordan's`). Ambiguity rules, choosing false negatives over false positives:
- "Turkey" counts only capitalised and not followed by a food word (dinner, roast, recipe, Thanksgiving within 3 words).
- "Georgia" counts only with Tbilisi, Georgian, or "Georgia's government/president/prime minister" nearby, and never with a US context word (Atlanta, governor, state, county, Senate race).
- "Jordan" and "Chad" count only when not directly preceded by a capitalised given-name-like word ("Michael Jordan", "Chad Smith" pattern: capitalised word + term, or term + capitalised surname).
- "Washington" is not a term (the US is matched by its names and aliases only).
- "Guinea" never matches inside "Guinea pig", "Papua New Guinea" (PG), "Equatorial Guinea" (GQ) or "Guinea-Bissau" (GW); longest-first ordering handles the latter three.
- "Congo" maps to CD unless "Brazzaville" or "Republic of the Congo" appears (then CG).
- "Niger" never matches inside "Nigeria" or "Nigerian" (whole-word boundary).

### Task 3: Snapshot builder (pure)

**Files:**
- Create: `backend/hotspots.py` (models and pure building; Task 4 adds the cache to the same file)
- Test: `backend/tests/test_hotspots_build.py`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `countries_named`, `COUNTRIES` and `country_terms` (Task 1); `Article` from `backend/models.py`.
- Produces (pydantic models, snake_case JSON):
  - `StoryArticle(title: str, url: str, outlet: str, published_at: datetime, image_url: str | None)`
  - `StoryImage(url: str, credit: str)`
  - `Story(headline: str, blurb: str | None, article: StoryArticle, image: StoryImage | None)`
  - `CountryHeat(iso2: str, iso_numeric: str, name: str, n: int, tier: Literal["quiet","active","elevated","high"], hourly: list[int], with_: list[str] = Field(alias="with"), story: Story | None)`, serialised with `by_alias=True`
  - `Cutoffs(active: int, elevated: int, high: int)`
  - `Hotspots(generated_at: datetime, window_hours: int = 24, coverage_hours: int, total_articles: int, feeds: int, cutoffs: Cutoffs, countries: list[CountryHeat])`
  - `merge_store(store: dict[str, Article], fresh: list[Article], now: datetime) -> dict[str, Article]`: keyed by `normalize_url(url)`; adds fresh entries, drops undated ones and anything published more than 24 hours before `now` (or more than 10 minutes in the future).
  - `build_snapshot(store: dict[str, Article], feeds: int, now: datetime) -> Hotspots`. Pure; images are left `None` (Task 4 adds them).

Rules, all from spec §3.3 and §3.5:
- **Title dedupe** inside `build_snapshot`: key = casefolded title with punctuation removed and a trailing ` - Outlet` stripped. Keep the newest.
- **`n`:** distinct articles naming the country (title + " " + snippet).
- **`cutoffs`:** `active = max(3, ceil(.015 * total))`, `elevated = max(8, ceil(.04 * total))`, `high = max(15, ceil(.08 * total))`.
- **`coverage_hours`:** for each outlet in the store, `now - oldest published_at` in whole hours, floored. Take the median across outlets, clamped to `[1, 24]`.
- **`hourly`:** length `coverage_hours`, oldest first; bucket `i` counts articles with `now - published_at` in `[(coverage_hours-1-i)h, (coverage_hours-i)h)`.
- **`with`:** other countries sharing at least 3 articles, top 3 by shared count (ties by iso2).
- **`story`:** for tiers active and above. The representative article is the one whose title's content words (lower-cased, length ≥ 3, not stopwords, not in `country_terms(iso2)`) overlap most, summed, with the country's other titles; ties go to the newer one. `headline` is that title (a trailing ` - Outlet` stripped). `blurb` is its snippet's first sentence, at most 30 words (cut at a word boundary and given `…`), or `None` when the snippet equals the title or is empty.
- **`countries`:** every country with `n ≥ 1`, sorted by `n` desc then name.

- Produces, in `grid.ts`:

```ts
export type GridDensity = 'desktop' | 'mobile'
export interface HotspotGrid {
  cols: number; rows: number            // desktop 132 cols; mobile 72 cols
  lngMin: -180; latMax: 80; cellDeg: number   // cellDeg = 360 / cols; rows = ceil(138 / cellDeg) (80°N to 58°S)
  cells: Record<string, number[]>       // iso_numeric -> cell indices (row * cols + col)
  anchors: Record<string, [number, number]>  // iso_numeric -> [col, row] in fractional cell units
}
export const GRIDS: Record<GridDensity, HotspotGrid>
```

Generator: use `world-atlas/countries-110m.json` with `topojson-client` `feature`, and the point-in-polygon from `build-land-points.mjs`. A cell belongs to the country containing its centre. A feature with an id but no cells gets the single cell nearest its largest polygon's bbox centre, if that cell is free. Features without an id are skipped. Anchors are the mean of the cell centres, except these overrides in [lng, lat]: US `840` [-98, 39], France `250` [2.5, 46.5], Russia `643` [60, 58], Canada `124` [-100, 58], Australia `036` [134, -25]. ```tsx

```tsx
}

```

Rendering, in SVG with a viewBox in cell units (`0 0 cols rows`), scaling to the container width:
1. The back grid: one `<path>` of hairlines on every cell boundary, `stroke: var(--ink)`, `stroke-opacity: .055`, with `vector-effect: non-scaling-stroke` and a width of 0.5.
2. Glow: for each High country, its cells as full-size squares, filled with its ramp colour, with an SVG blur (about 7px equivalent) at opacity .35.
3. Countries: one `<g data-iso>` per country with cells. Each cell is a square of side `cellScale(t)`, centred in its cell and filled `heatColour(t)`. Countries absent from the snapshot use `n = 0`. With a null snapshot, every cell is `--rule-strong` at side .5 (the skeleton).
4. Lift: when `active` is a non-quiet country, render a top layer containing the slot (its full cells at `--ink` 7%), a blurred shadow, and a copy of its cells. Toggle the `up` class on the next frame so CSS runs the 450ms lift (translate −12px in screen terms, scale 1.07, shadow offset 6/12 and opacity .3). On leave, drop `up` and unmount after 450ms. Everything except the active country fades to opacity .4.
5. Hit layer: one transparent `<path>` per country made of its full-size cells, never transformed, carrying pointer handlers. Quiet countries get `cursor: default`.
6. Enter animation: on the first render with data, each country `<g>` gets a delay of `rank(tier) × 220ms + random × 260ms` (quiet first), fading in and dropping 12px over 900ms. Skipped when `reduced`.
7. Coordinate readout (desktop only): a corner `<div aria-hidden>` updated on `pointermove` without a React render (as `applyReadout` does). Equirectangular invert: `lng = -180 + x * cellDeg`, `lat = 80 - y * cellDeg`.
8. The SVG gets `role="img"` and an `aria-label` built from the top three countries (spec §9).
